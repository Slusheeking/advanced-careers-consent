Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2026

Advanced Careers ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you provide when interacting with our recruiting communications, including SMS/text messaging services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name, email address, and mobile phone number
  • Job application details and resume content
  • Communication preferences and opt-in/opt-out status

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Send updates about your job application status
  • Schedule and confirm interviews
  • Respond to your inquiries related to open positions
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

3. SMS / Text Messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes at any time. Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared, sold, rented, or otherwise disclosed to any third party. All other categories of information may be shared in accordance with this Privacy Policy, but never for marketing purposes originating from a third party.

If you provide your mobile phone number and consent to receive text messages, Advanced Careers will send you SMS messages related solely to your recruiting and job application activity. Message frequency varies based on application activity. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, you will receive a single confirmation message and no further texts. To re-enroll, reply START. For help, reply HELP or contact us at zach@getmeslushed.com.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share information only with service providers who assist us in operating our recruiting pipeline (e.g., SMS delivery platforms), and only as necessary to deliver those services. These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and may not use your data for their own purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, to comply with legal obligations, or until you withdraw consent. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at zach@getmeslushed.com.

6. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at zach@getmeslushed.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of our services after any change constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
